Privacy

What we keep. Your email address, your profile, the opinions and reviews you post, and photos you upload to your own listings.

Leases and receipts. When you upload a lease page or rent receipt to verify a review, a person checks it once and the file is then deleted. We keep only the result (verified or not).

What others see. Opinions and reviews show your role (for example "Past tenant") and the year, not your name or email.

Deleting your account removes your profile, opinions and reviews. Do it from Profile, then Delete account.
